# Fit Preferences

- **Target roles:** Role one, Role two
- **Level:** Years of experience and the level you want. Roles you do not want (for example director, intern).
- **Work authorization:** Per country: citizen / permit, and whether you need sponsorship.
- **Location:** Cities or countries, on-site / hybrid / remote, relocation.
- **Work languages:** Language (level).
- **Dealbreakers:** Anything that rules a job out (night shifts, on-call, no sponsorship, ...).
